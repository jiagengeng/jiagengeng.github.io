Research

Ph.D. Tire Wear Particles

Tire wear particles release 6PPD, an antioxidant in nearly every tire. Its ozonation product, 6PPD-quinone, reaches streams in stormwater and is acutely lethal to coho salmon. I develop electrochemical strategies to remove both, built around modular, electrified treatment units.

M.S. Antibiotics

Antibiotics from farming and medicine end up in rivers, soils and the crops grown on them. I studied where they accumulate and what risks they pose, and how to degrade them with modified biochar-catalyzed and UV-based advanced oxidation, including how dissolved organic matter changes its efficiency. I quantified norfloxacin by HPLC, on an Agilent 1100 almost as old as I am.
